# Citation and evidence scope

The report is an architecture and transition blueprint. Source classes are separated rather than silently merged:

1. **TOM source-derived material** — the canonical 244-byte seed, TOMAGI 1.0 formal substrate and TOM-SRS 1.0 seeded referential substrate supplied with the project.
2. **Companion-report material** — the preceding Manufacturing Horizons report, included in `references/`.
3. **Current external factual grounding** — official Google product pages, Alphabet/SEC reporting, and public protocol/commons documentation listed in the report's references.
4. **Architectural synthesis and inference** — the proposed TOM Commons constitution, service modules, human-needs genome, journeys, transition roadmap, metrics and risk register.

The report does not claim affiliation with Google or Alphabet, a deployed replacement service, AGI, universal knowledge, global distributed consensus, autonomous medical authority or completed biological manufacturing. The phrase “prehistoric present day” is treated as criticism of obsolete institutional interfaces and incentive structures, not of the human worth of people, professions, cultures or genders.
