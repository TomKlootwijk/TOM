TOM.SEQ & ordered composition & operators\textasciicircum{}n -> operator & Composition is serialized and non-commutative;\allowbreak{} execute the stored order without re-sorting. \\
TOM.INDEX0 & zero-based position & offset -> ordinal & ordinal = offset + 1;\allowbreak{} the stored timeline coordinate remains the zero-based offset. \\
TOM.LOGPOLAR & log-polar chart & (x,\allowbreak{}y,\allowbreak{}r0) -> (rho,\allowbreak{}theta) & rho=ln(r/\allowbreak{}r0),\allowbreak{} theta=atan2(y,\allowbreak{}x);\allowbreak{} runtime consumes quantized rho/\allowbreak{}theta already compiled. \\

TOM.PARITY & one-bit reduction & u32 -> bit & P(w)=popcount(w) mod 2. \\
TOM.JIT1 & one-bit jitter & seed x K x X -> \{-a,\allowbreak{}+a\} & j=P(mix32(seed xor K\_hi xor rotl(K\_lo,\allowbreak{}13) xor X xor aux));\allowbreak{} sigma=2j-1;\allowbreak{} selected state field += sigma*a. \\

TOM.DELTA2 & second discrete difference & v\_n,\allowbreak{}v\_(n-1) -> a\_n & Delta\textasciicircum{}2 q\_n=v\_n-v\_(n-1);\allowbreak{} Delta\textasciicircum{}2 phi is the angular acceleration coordinate. \\
TOM.KIN2 & second-order update & state x acceleration-cell -> state & v\_(n+1)=v\_n+a\_cell;\allowbreak{} q\_(n+1)=q\_n+v\_(n+1). \\
TOM.PHI & lower-case phi hinge & phi x delta -> phi & phi is periodic in Z/\allowbreak{}(2\textasciicircum{}12 Z);\allowbreak{} capital Phi is reserved for the golden ratio and is not the runtime phase. \\
TOM.TIME & timeline phase & X x delta -> X,\allowbreak{}winding & X is a 14-bit modular tick;\allowbreak{} wraps contribute to lineage while the tick remains periodic. \\
TOM.CONE & analytic cone relation & packed chart x cone parameters -> signed relation & r=max(rho\_min-rho,\allowbreak{}rho-rho\_max,\allowbreak{}|cyclic(theta-theta0)|-alpha\_q);\allowbreak{} inside iff r<=0. \\
TOM.SWEEP & swept cone family & cone x path -> relation & F\_sweep(x)=inf\_u d\_C(x-s(u));\allowbreak{} a compiled profile may tabulate the path as KIN2 cells. \\
TOM.CIRCLE & circle/\allowbreak{}radial shell & rho x center,\allowbreak{}width -> signed relation & A circle is a radial shell in the packed chart and an axial projection of a cone. \\
TOM.SPHERE & spherical support & rho,\allowbreak{}phi x center,\allowbreak{}width -> signed relation & Packed shell residual is max(|rho-rho0|-dr,\allowbreak{} |cyclic(phi-phi0)|-dphi);\allowbreak{} Euclidean helper is ||x-c||-R. \\
TOM.OVERLAP & lens intersection & A,\allowbreak{}B -> A intersect B & Shared domain is exact conjunction/\allowbreak{}intersection;\allowbreak{} it may be used as a join key or connector locus. \\
TOM.KLEIN & reflective Klein wrap & state -> state & On an odd radial wrap: rho'=rho mod N\_rho,\allowbreak{} theta'=N\_theta/\allowbreak{}2-theta,\allowbreak{} phi'=-phi,\allowbreak{} orientation'=orientation xor 1;\allowbreak{} optional sheet flip. \\
TOM.MOBIUS & half-turn bundle & state -> state & On an odd wrap: theta'=theta+N\_theta/\allowbreak{}2,\allowbreak{} phi'=-phi,\allowbreak{} orientation'=orientation xor 1. \\
TOM.RADIX & radix branch & K x bit-index -> bit & Select one canonical key bit;\allowbreak{} repeated selections form a radix-trie path. \\
TOM.HINGE & typed hinge & branch x state x map -> state & H=(pivot/\allowbreak{}state,\allowbreak{}map0,\allowbreak{}map1,\allowbreak{}invariant);\allowbreak{} runtime map0 is identity and map1 is the cell's declared delta plus optional orientation/\allowbreak{}sheet flip. \\
TOM.LSYS & binary L-system step & branch x state -> state & Apply chirality-signed turn and dyadic velocity scaling,\allowbreak{} corresponding to F(T)->F(T/\allowbreak{}2)[+/\allowbreak{}-phi F(T/\allowbreak{}2)]. \\
TOM.BRANCH & binary successor & bit x cell -> cell & i\_(n+1)=next\_i[bit]. \\
TOM.TRANSITION & state patch & state x cell -> state & Execute exactly one opcode,\allowbreak{} normalize periodic fields,\allowbreak{} select successor,\allowbreak{} and update lineage. \\
TOM.PROJECT & symbolic projection & state x tag -> tag & Emit a side-view pyramid,\allowbreak{} circle,\allowbreak{} sphere,\allowbreak{} action,\allowbreak{} class,\allowbreak{} or other downstream token without making projection the substrate. \\
TOM.EMIT & deterministic output & state x payload -> state & Set the output token and emitted bit;\allowbreak{} optional halt is a program semantic,\allowbreak{} not a safety mode. \\
TOM.LINEAGE & replay lineage & state x cell x key -> u32 & ell'=mix32(ell xor payload xor aux xor K\_hi xor rotl(K\_lo,\allowbreak{}7) xor branch xor cell\_index). \\
TOM.TRACE & explanation trace & execution -> records & Return ordered cell IDs/\allowbreak{}opcodes,\allowbreak{} branches,\allowbreak{} keys,\allowbreak{} residuals,\allowbreak{} outputs and lineage. \\
TOM.ACTIVE & active-bit projection & integer -> set(bit positions) & A(n)=\{k | bit\_k(n)=1\};\allowbreak{} the original numeric value is retained. \\
TOM.POPCOUNT & active-bit cardinality & bit set -> integer & Return the number of active positions in a finite binary word. \\
TOM.EQ & typed equality predicate & A x A -> bit & Return 1 exactly when two values of the same declared type are equal;\allowbreak{} the result is a branch bit,\allowbreak{} not an identity rewrite. \\
