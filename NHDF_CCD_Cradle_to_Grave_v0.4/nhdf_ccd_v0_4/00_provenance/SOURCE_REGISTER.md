# Source register and evidence boundary

## Supplied sources

- **S1 - Original operator-chain transcript (30 pages).** Introduces parity, lower-case phi, log-polar LUTs, kinematics, cone sweep, projections, BST/L-system branching, a Klein-bottle/SDF-zero idea, and GPU/optical/SAR extensions.
- **S2 - NHDF Formal Specification v0.1 (26 pages).** Recasts global `SDF = 0` as non-degenerate local constraints, separates data parity from topology parity, bounds recursion and memory, and defines a causal update chain.
- **S3 - Commute design transcript (40 pages).** Adds the log-polar Jacobian insight, register-level metaphors, and an early claim that SDF plus kinematics solves CCD. The v0.4 package retains the insight but rejects the one-read/one-bit completeness claim.
- **S4 - Spherical-Cartesian/whole-substrate transcript (70 pages).** Extends the vocabulary to measurement, sensors, actuators, chemistry, biology, medicine, photonics, cross-Kerr, fabrication, SAR, and cosmology. These are retained only as separate adapter hypotheses.

## Evidence rules

1. Source-derived vocabulary is preserved where it can be given a typed, testable meaning.
2. Unsupported source claims are not silently repaired into facts. They are marked **reinterpreted**, **rejected**, or **adapter hypothesis**.
3. CCD correctness is determined by geometric predicates, conservative bounds, exact or inclusion-based solvers, explicit tolerances, and failure states. Parity, golden-ratio splits, and topology metadata cannot overrule those predicates.
4. Personal identifiers are not executable state, cryptographic material, or validation evidence.
