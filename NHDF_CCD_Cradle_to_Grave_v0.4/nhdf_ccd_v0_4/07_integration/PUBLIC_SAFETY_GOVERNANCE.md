# Public-safety and autonomous-agent governance

A police/emergency-services adapter may use CCD for vehicle motion planning, robot collision avoidance, camera rig geometry, or evidence-system mechanical safety. It does not justify automated suspicion, identity inference, use-of-force selection, or covert surveillance.

Minimum governance gates:

- documented lawful purpose and data minimization;
- human authority over consequential decisions;
- independent bias, privacy, cybersecurity, and safety review;
- immutable audit logs using conventional cryptography;
- explicit uncertainty and safe-stop behavior;
- red-team testing for spoofing, occlusion, sensor failure, and adversarial scenes;
- retention and deletion schedules.
