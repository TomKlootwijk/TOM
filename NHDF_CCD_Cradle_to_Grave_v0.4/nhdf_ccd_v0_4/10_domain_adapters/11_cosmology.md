# Cosmology and holography adapter

The current mapping is terminological only. To become physics, it must specify a bulk theory, a boundary theory, observables, degrees of freedom, dynamics, entropy, and a demonstrable duality or new prediction.

A projected SDF or a data boundary is not the holographic principle. CCD may still be used inside numerical relativity or astrophysical simulations to detect mesh/contact/event surfaces, but that computational role does not establish an ontological substrate.
