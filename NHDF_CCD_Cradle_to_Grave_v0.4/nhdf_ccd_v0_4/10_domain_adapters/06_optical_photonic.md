# Optical and photonic computing adapter

Optical hardware can implement selected linear transforms, convolutions, Fourier/log-polar mappings, and routing transfer functions. The rigorous claim is operator equivalence: a measured physical transfer approximates a declared numerical operator within error.

A first NHDF experiment should implement only encoding plus a local constraint/projection stage. It must measure phase/amplitude error, drift, bandwidth, calibration cost, energy including electro-optic conversion, and repeatability.

An optical device cannot be the sole safety authority for CCD until its error envelope is conservative under temperature, fabrication variation, source noise, detector noise, and aging. A photonic accelerator may propose a TOI interval; a digital certificate layer should verify it.
