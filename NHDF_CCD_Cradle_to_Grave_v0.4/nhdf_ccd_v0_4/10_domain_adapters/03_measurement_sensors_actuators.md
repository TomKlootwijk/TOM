# Measurement, sensor, and actuator adapter

The supplied material correctly gravitates toward a general measurement chain: physical quantity, transduction, representation, calibration, and interpretation. NHDF can standardize the state and event interface, but each instrument retains units, calibration, traceability, uncertainty, bandwidth, hysteresis, drift, and environmental sensitivity.

CCD applies literally to moving mechanisms and safety envelopes: motor stages, calipers, robotic arms, shutters, valves, scanners, and optical elements. Other threshold crossings can reuse interval bracketing, but should be named boundary-event detection rather than collision detection.

An actuator response is governed by electromechanical, hydraulic, thermal, or material dynamics. A parity event may request a safe mode; it is not a physical restoring law.
