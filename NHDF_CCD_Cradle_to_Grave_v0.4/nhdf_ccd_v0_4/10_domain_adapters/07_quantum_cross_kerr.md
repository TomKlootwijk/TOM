# Quantum and cross-Kerr adapter

A valid quantum profile needs a Hilbert space, preparation rule, Hamiltonian or channel, gate sequence, measurement, noise model, resource count, and classical comparison. Writing a photon-number parity expression is not enough.

Cross-Kerr interactions are a recognizable route to conditional phase shifts, but realistic traveling-wave gates face response, loss, mode-overlap, group-velocity, and phase-noise constraints. The v0.4 roadmap therefore treats cross-Kerr parity as an experiment proposal, not as a component of the CCD correctness chain and not as a biological mechanism.
