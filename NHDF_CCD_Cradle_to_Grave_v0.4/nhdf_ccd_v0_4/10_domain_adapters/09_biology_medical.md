# Biology and medical adapter

CCD is useful in biomechanical simulation: tissue-tool contact, joint surfaces, blood-cell/device contact, catheter navigation, surgical planning, and anatomical mesh self-collision. These are geometric simulation tasks with material and fluid models layered above detection.

Physiological boundaries, vascular branching, neural signaling, ultraweak photon emission, and possible axonal waveguiding are separate scientific questions. They cannot be replaced by SDF, parity, golden-ratio, or cross-Kerr terminology.

The package makes no claim to prevent disease, regenerate tissue, reverse dementia, restore swallowing, or make organisms crash-proof. Any diagnostic or therapeutic claim requires a defined device/intervention, mechanism, uncertainty, preclinical replication, clinical trials, ethics, and regulatory review.
