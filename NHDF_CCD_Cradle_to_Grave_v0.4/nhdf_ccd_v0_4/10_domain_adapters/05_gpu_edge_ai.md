# GPU and edge-AI adapter

For CCD, GPUs are a direct acceleration target: broad phase, backend bucketing, batched exact predicates, inclusion tests, queue compaction, and earliest-TOI reduction.

For AI, log-polar coding, local constraints, sparse routing, and bounded feedback may be studied as a codec or architecture. This has no automatic relationship to collision correctness. A learned model may rank or propose candidate pairs, but a safety profile must conservatively retain all pairs or verify discarded pairs with a certified bound.

Metrics must include quality, false-negative rate, latency distribution, memory, energy, thermal throttling, and comparison with equal-budget quantization/sparsity baselines.
